## Important: Use Your Personal Email Address

> **Please join using your personal Google account (`@gmail.com`).**

**Why?**  
Institutional or university Google accounts (such as college-managed Google Workspace IDs) often have domain restrictions. These restrictions block students from joining classes outside their organization domain or trigger "Class not found" errors. Using your personal Gmail account ensures you can join smoothly without permissions issues.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Cause | What to do |
|---------|-------|------------|
| **"Class not found" or "Code invalid"** | You are signed in with your university or college Google account. | Sign out or open an Incognito / Private window, sign in with your personal `@gmail.com` account, and try again. |
| **"Wrong account"** | Browser defaulted to another saved Google profile. | Click your profile picture at the top right of Google Classroom and switch to your personal Gmail account. |
| **Typo in code** | Extra space or wrong case. | Copy and paste `txz4xibu` directly. All letters are lowercase. |
